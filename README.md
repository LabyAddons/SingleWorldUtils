# SingleWorldUtils

> **Your worlds. Your way.**
> Small utilities that make your singleplayer experience a little better.

SingleWorldUtils is a lightweight addon built around one simple idea: **making singleplayer worlds more convenient without getting in your way.**

### ✨ What it does

* **⏸️ Pause Control** — Decide whether your world should continue running when opening the pause menu.
* **⌚ World Playtime** — See how long you've played each world directly in the world selection.
* **🖼️ World Icons** — Automatically refresh your world icon whenever you enter the world.

### 📦 Installation

1. Press `Win` + `R`
2. Paste:
   `%appdata%/.minecraft/LabyMod-neo/addons`
3. Put the [SingleWorldUtils.jar](https://github.com/LabyAddons/SingleWorldUtils/releases/latest/download/SingleWorldUtils.jar) into the folder.
4. Start LabyMod.

### 💡 Why SingleWorldUtils?

Minecraft gives you the worlds — **SingleWorldUtils gives you a little more control over them.**
No unnecessary changes. No bloated menus. Just a collection of small features designed around your singleplayer worlds.
